        About PCare AI
      

      

        PCare AI is an intelligent healthcare Suggestion platform built to help patients in India make informed decisions
        about their health — instantly. Powered by Google's Gemini AI, it analyses symptoms and provides
        real-time guidance on urgency level and appropriate next steps.
      

      

        Our system uses a combination of deterministic red-flag detection and advanced AI reasoning to categorise
        symptoms into Self-Care, OPD, or Emergency triage levels. We also surface nearby verified clinics in Bengaluru
        so you never have to search alone during a health crisis.
      

      

        Designed with privacy at its core — your symptom data is not stored or shared. PCare AI is a decision-support
        tool, not a replacement for professional medical consultation.
